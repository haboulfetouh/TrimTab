Questions

What teams ask first.

Is it safe to give Trimtab access?

You start read-only, so the worst case is a number on a dashboard — never a change to production. When you’re ready to let it act, every action is previewed, approved, logged and reversible.

Which clouds do you support?

All five major clouds: AWS, Azure, GCP, OCI and Huawei Cloud — unified into a single view using the open FOCUS billing standard.

Do you store our credentials?

No. Trimtab uses cross-account roles and service accounts with zero stored secrets, and can run entirely inside your own VPC.

How fast is the audit?

Most teams see their first ranked savings within 30 minutes of connecting — no installation, no production changes.

What does it cost?

The audit is free. The platform is priced against the value it delivers — a share of what we actually save you — so it pays for itself by design.

Who is it for?

Mid-market and enterprise teams running real multi-cloud spend, whether or not you have a dedicated FinOps function.
